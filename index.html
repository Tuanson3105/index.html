Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about the Focus Protocol mechanics.

What is Focus Protocol?

Focus Protocol is a Web3 productivity dApp built on the Nimiq network. It solves procrastination by using financial collateral. You stake NIM crypto to start a work timer. If you complete it, your money is returned. If you cheat or quit, your money is liquidated.

How does the Anti-Cheat tracking work?

The smart contract enforces a "Proof of Attention" mechanism. By utilizing the browser's native API, the application monitors your active tab. If you minimize the browser or switch to another tab (e.g., YouTube, Twitter), the contract detects the blur event and immediately fails the session.

Where do liquidated funds go?

100% of liquidated funds (from users who forfeit or cheat) are sent to a community treasury. These funds are algorithmically distributed to the top users on the Leaderboard as a reward for extreme discipline, or used to burn NIM to support the ecosystem.

Is the Smart Contract audited?

Yes. The core staking and liquidation logic utilizes the official Nimiq Hub API and standard escrow contracts, which have been thoroughly audited by top-tier security firms. Your funds are never held by a centralized third party.

What happens if I lose my internet connection?

The protocol includes a 60-second grace period for network drops. As long as you do not close the tab or switch windows, the timer will resync with the blockchain once your connection is restored.

Are there any protocol fees?

No. Focus Protocol charges a 0% fee for successful sessions. You get exactly what you staked back. The platform sustains itself entirely through the liquidated collateral of users who fail their sessions.
